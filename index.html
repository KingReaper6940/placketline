FROM SCREEN TO FABRIC

Measure the paper first.

The PDF has physical dimensions. Your printer settings can change them. The screen overview does not.

  1. Print on the selected paper at 100% / Actual size. Disable fit, shrink and duplex.
  2. Measure both 100 mm calibration bars with a real ruler. Reject the printout if either is wrong.
  3. Cut along the strip edges. Overlap adjacent strips by matching both labeled join lines and the centerline. Each shared run is 10 mm.
  4. Check the assembled band length and every center. Transfer marks only after comparing with your actual garment and pattern.

Centers only. Your pattern decides the rest.

These crosses are button centers, not buttonhole starts, ends or presser-foot alignment marks. Placketline does not choose button count, predict fit or gaping, size buttonholes, or verify fabric strength. Anchors can create different gaps in different sections. It cannot repair an already cut buttonhole. Test your actual materials and follow your pattern.

Exact fractions are kept in the receipt. Screen and CSV decimals are rounded to 0.001 mm; PDF drawing coordinates are rounded to 0.000001 points. Printed accuracy depends on your printer, assembly and measurements. Very close centers retain all crosses but suppress button labels in the PDF.
